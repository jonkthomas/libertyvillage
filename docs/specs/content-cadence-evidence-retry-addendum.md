## Trigger and source of truth

Only a **normal blog attempt with a validated pack and no attached submission** can enter this path. The trusted runner verifies there is no new post. The root helper relays exactly one allowlisted, EOF-finalized `{postWritten:false,stopReason}` from its existing bounded generator diagnostic, using a fresh root-owned invocation-bound channel rather than scratch files or raw prose. Only `unsupported-grounding` and `insufficient-sources` trigger the pass; `sdk-error`, `generator-error`, `no-post-unspecified`, `duplicate`, absent/malformed/duplicate/contradictory outcomes, timeouts and nonzero unit errors do not. The writer's regex-derived enum is **not evidence**: it merely authorizes bounded discovery. A post, even with a refusal enum, still goes through the existing binding and gate path or is refused as contradictory; no new bypass exists.

Trusted pre-spend `checkTopicGroundability`/`buildSourcePack` skips are **not** this retry: they have no verified pack or attempt. They remain skipped without writer spend. Unsupported operational premises such as pet-friendly or happy-hour are never inferred from a Maps query, a generic description, a model, a web snippet or an address. This change does not add first-party policy-proof ingestion; those topics stay skipped or blocked for the week and the runner advances to other eligible topics. A gate-rejected/blocked blog submission is also not an evidence trigger; pending publication resumes its original key.

1. Close the refused attempt `failed-before-submit` (no submission) under its fenced content lease, preserve its immutable key/digest/history, then release that lease. A crash before registering a discovery claim may forfeit the opportunity this week; it must not invent a receipt or repeat an ambiguous source call.
2. With no content-slot lease held, and under the existing outer VM `global.lock` (never a nested launcher invocation), atomically claim one DB-backed evidence entitlement for this **target, ISO week and intent fingerprint** before any source call. Losers neither call sources nor close active work. At most one discovery pass per fingerprint/week and at most one per weekly-blog run. No invented target-wide weekly `E` cap. If the two current-live content posts already exist, skip discovery. Preserve existing weekly-miss alerts.
3. Select at most one existing directory category from the _trusted original source pack_ if its records unambiguously map to the static discovery category allowlist. Extend `discover-businesses.mjs` with an allowlisted category selector, bounded `--max` (no more than 3 additions), finite per-request and overall source deadlines, and a structured bounded outcome that distinguishes relevant Maps outage from genuine no-change. This pass runs with `sourceEnv('discover-businesses')` only, not generator/DB/deploy credentials. Optional Pexels image failure is not a Maps evidence outage; lack of required media still follows the unchanged business gate. No supported category means no source call and a closed-for-week topic. Never treat newly generated descriptions or query words as proof of an operational premise.
4. Submit the directory diff with one immutable discovery idempotency key and pass the **unchanged** business gate, deploy, and current-revision item smoke. `lookup` by the key before interpreting any lost reply; a pending, gated, published-but-not-smoked, compensated or transport-error submission is **not** accepted evidence and is resumed or left pending on that key. Gate rejection yields no evidence; true source/gate/transport outage is an operational result, not a grounded refusal. A 200 response or DB `published` alone does not prove a directory link is live.
5. After successful propagation, take a fresh trusted export and rerun `checkTopicGroundability`, `buildSourcePack` and `verifySourcePack`. Only if the rebuilt pack is verified, **different from the original digest**, and grounded for the original title may the runner make one new attempt for the same topic. A genuinely empty pass, irrelevant new records, unsupported premise or unchanged pack closes the topic for this week without another generation. Do not soften the title, publish filler or reset writer/model/independent gate budgets.
6. The second attempt uses the same topic key/intent fingerprint but a **different unused content slot** (<= existing maxContentSlot 4), a fresh ordinal/key and its own immutable new pack digest. `cadence_distinct_intent` in migration 0003 forbids reusing the original slot. Fenced DB state ties at most one new retry attempt to the evidence entitlement, including its digest; reconcile linked or orphaned same-fingerprint attempts before allocating. Recheck current-live count and pending work after discovery. If no slot exists, expire the retry safely rather than raise slot limits. Include linked slots 3/4 in restart reconciliation even though the ordinary goal-driven loop may not visit them. Pending retries keep their original key through crash/rollover and obey late-smoke rules. Another grounded refusal on that attempt closes the topic for **this ISO week**; a model/provider outage does not label it unsupported and cannot create a third attempt.

Reads/writes are target-scoped and fenced/conditional. The row is a **weekly** spending and exclusion marker, not a permanent user-visible ban or a second publishing ledger. A crash after claim but before source may close unused for this week rather than rerun ambiguously. On recovery, always check the immutable discovery lookup before calling sources again or treating a claimed row as unused; never abandon an attached/pending submission as a grounded block.
