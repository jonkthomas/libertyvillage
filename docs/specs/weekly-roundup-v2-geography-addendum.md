## Boundary findings and owner policy choices

1. **Douro is an explicit street-specific A1 exception, not an implied polygon hole.** Eleven City Douro address points and four Douro rows lie inside the ring, although approved §6.2 requires Douro `not-LV` and calls it absent from the table. The raw points remain for provenance, but `classifyAddress` rejects Douro and the four road rows cannot qualify. The nearby Shaw/Crawford/Canniff/Solidarity pocket remains core under the approved point-in-polygon address rule. Excluding that pocket as a whole would be an unapproved new rule; the owner deliberately follows the spec's narrower named exception.
2. **Address points and road frontage have different classes under approved §6.2.** A City address point strictly inside the ring is core with trusted Toronto context, including 38 King St W, six Strachan Ave and 15 Dufferin St points. Boundary *road segments* are adjacent, not core. The previous address-frontage demotion was withdrawn. An exact City number+street and Toronto provenance are still necessary; a street name or an IG account alone is not admission.
3. **Intersection-bound road records, not corridor labels.** A road record must name a road and two intersections on constituent City rows of one class. `atRoad` can supply both intersections; conflicting matches are `unverifiable`. A core row with both cross roads on boundary roads, such as Mowat King→King, is ambiguous and held. A point over 150 m from the matched segment rejects the record, never admits it. The actual A1 records R09 Strachan Fleet→Fleet, R53 King Strachan→Strachan and R54 Lake Shore Newfoundland→Martin Goodman Trl are adjacent; so is King Atlantic→Jefferson.
4. **Four adjacent frontage corridors, not five overlapping paths.** King St W Strachan→Dufferin (17 rows), Strachan Ave King→Lake Shore (12), Dufferin St King→Saskatchewan Rd (10), and Lake Shore Blvd W Strachan→British Columbia Rd (nine: `30125351`, `30021327`, `30021270`, `30021271`, `30016282`, `60024495`, `30016243`, `9234087`, `1147551`). Strachan Fleet→King is already part of the longer Strachan path and is not a fifth corridor. Dufferin does not meet Lake Shore in this City graph. British Columbia Rd (node `13469154`, longitude −79.42918) is the first named Lake Shore intersection at or west of Dufferin's longitude; it is the reviewed western proxy for §6.2's impossible named endpoint. The parallel Lake Shore row `1147552` to a BC ramp node is **not** included. This proxy is the owner's specific implementation choice, subject to independent review.
5. **Exhibition Place internal roads are adjacent only by exact IDs.** A grounds ring joins City C N R Strachan→Dufferin, Dufferin to Saskatchewan, British Columbia Rd to Lake Shore, Lake Shore to Strachan and Strachan back to C N R. Thirty-two wholly inside road/Other/Pending rows were reviewed: Alberta Crcl, British Columbia Rd, Canada Blvd, Manitoba Dr, New Brunswick Way, Newfoundland Rd, Nova Scotia Ave, Nunavut Rd, Ontario Dr north of Lake Shore, Prince Edward Island Cres, Princes' Blvd, Quebec St, Saskatchewan Rd and Yukon Pl. Trails, ramps, expressways, bridges and boundary roads do not enter the internal list; south-of-Lake-Shore Remembrance/Ontario Place and east-of-Strachan Stadium/Fort York/Fleet are not admitted. The generator re-derives and checks this ID list.
6. **Instagram watch: 30 verified core handles, four excluded.** `louiecoffeeshop` joins after its first-party `louiecoffee.com` link verified the handle and the City table placed 1187 King inside the ring. `kitchenhub` (1108 King outside), `tonton.matcha.coffee` (no first-party handle proof), `brodflour` (8 Pardee missing from City points) and `DeltaTrainLV` (37A Mowat is not 37 Mowat) were examined; these are four excluded handles after Louie was restored.

## Identities and admission consequences

Lamport Stadium has one approved canonical identity `addr:75-fraser-ave` with the stadium/parking-lot name aliases; City's 1155 King Allan A. Lamport Stadium Park point is **not** the stadium identity. Exhibition Place grounds have their own `grounds:exhibition-place` ID, distinct from Enercare Centre at `addr:100-princes-blvd`. A contradictory stated name and address is `unverifiable`, never silently merged. `QUEST XO Chocolate Creative Lab` is an exact registered venue alias; unreviewed `+ Creative Lab` spelling is not inferred. City-internal sub-suffixes such as 3AA, 15RR and 1007BB (78 pairs) cannot be parsed from prose and fail closed. Solidarity Way points coexist with Centreline rows still named Canniff St; no fuzzy equivalence is inferred.

Core named venues in prose require same-record Toronto context or the official venue domain. The namesake guard rejects a comma-plus-US-state abbreviation/name or an abbreviation-plus-ZIP; it is not a general US geocoder (bare written-out states without a comma are not covered). Route 504 requires a reviewed Strachan–Dufferin King stop, not bare `King St W`; parsed named endpoints outside the reviewed King-frontage allowlist reject it. The Instagram single-location own-venue fallback refuses pinned other places, recognizable street addresses, and `at`/`in`/`on` place phrases that do not resolve to the account's own building; the finite negative recognizer is not a general natural-language place parser. A pinned same-record own-venue label or address may resolve to the watch account's verified building but cannot override a contradictory offsite statement. A bare `📍 Liberty Village` neither establishes locality nor changes the account's reviewed own-venue rule. A City project or BIA source can establish Toronto context for its own exact address-bearing record, but it cannot make an unlocated project meeting local or bypass the named-venue prose rule. All item evidence remains bound to one freshly re-extracted section or post block.
